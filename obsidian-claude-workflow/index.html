Day-to-day: what it actually does for me

Morning admin
> Set up today.
It reads my calendar feed, writes the daily note, opens a prepped note per meeting, links each to earlier calls in the series, and drafts an agenda from the vault.
Action tracking
> Zurich are holding until Q4. Update the action.
It finds the action, appends the update, rewrites the summary, moves the due date. 132 actions stay current without a tracker.
Comms in my voice
> Draft the weekly update for eSIM.
It pulls the week from action files and call notes, then drafts to the house rules: short, punchy, non-technical, three bullets. My edits go back into the rulebook, so drafts keep converging on what I would send.
Structured product thinking
> We want to add screen protection to tickets. Walk me through this properly.
A skill runs it through the full workflow: intake, framing, goals, personas, JTBD, prioritisation, solution, roadmap. It pauses for me at each stage and ends with a branded deck on a link I can send round.

I supply judgement. They supply memory, structure and first drafts. Everything lands back in my system, in my format, ready for the next conversation.

From vague ask to roadmap: the initiative process

One sentence from a stakeholder triggers eleven stages of product discovery, on the Product Institute's methodology, each pausing for my judgement.

> We want to add screen protection to tickets. Walk me through this properly.
01 · INTAKE
Understand the ask
It grills me for what a one-line ask never carries: why now, what success looks like, what is sensitive. Then framed against strategy, with a measurable goal.
02 · DISCOVERY
Understand the people
Stakeholder mapping, personas and empathy maps for the customers affected, then Jobs-to-be-Done to find the real problem. Asks are often a solution in disguise.
03 · VALIDATION
Check it is worth it
Scored against customer signal and business alignment, then the riskiest assumptions get cheap tests before anything is built.
04 · DEFINITION
Define and commit
Solution exploration, a story map, the minimum feature set, and a roadmap entry with outcomes and priority.
FINALE
Stakeholder deck
Generated from the artifacts, published to a link I can send round.

Every stage saves its artifact into the vault. Screen Protection alone produced 15: strategy canvas, personas, job stories, story map, roadmap. Personas reuse across initiatives, so the process compounds.

Discovery discipline I would struggle to sustain alone, in hours rather than weeks, and none of it lives in my head or a chat window.
